# Getting help

- **Found a bug or have a question about a specific project?** Open an issue in that project's repository.
- **Want to reuse, adapt or deploy one of our tools in your city or country?** Email [hello@opencitieslab.org](mailto:hello@opencitieslab.org). We'd love to hear from you.
- **Interested in partnering with us?** Visit [opencitieslab.org](https://www.opencitieslab.org/) or email [hello@opencitieslab.org](mailto:hello@opencitieslab.org).
- **Security issue?** Follow our [Security Policy](SECURITY.md) and don't open a public issue.

Our team is small, so we can't guarantee support for every repository, especially older ones under [`opendatadurban`](https://github.com/opendatadurban). We do read everything.
