# Security Policy

Open Cities Lab builds systems used by governments and residents, so we take security and data protection seriously.

## Reporting a vulnerability

**Please do not open a public issue for security vulnerabilities.**

Instead, either:

- use GitHub's **[private vulnerability reporting](https://docs.github.com/en/code-security/security-advisories/guidance-on-reporting-and-writing-information-about-vulnerabilities/privately-reporting-a-security-vulnerability)** ("Report a vulnerability" under the repository's **Security** tab), where enabled, or
- email **[hello@opencitieslab.org](mailto:hello@opencitieslab.org)** with the subject line `SECURITY: <repository name>`.

Please include:

- the affected repository and version, commit or URL
- a description of the issue and its potential impact
- steps to reproduce, or a proof of concept
- whether any personal or government data may be exposed

## What to expect

- We will acknowledge your report as soon as we can.
- We will investigate, keep you updated and let you know when a fix is available.
- We are happy to credit you once the issue is resolved, if you'd like.

Please give us reasonable time to fix the issue before disclosing it publicly, and do not access, modify or delete data that isn't yours while you investigate.

## Scope

This policy covers the repositories in the [`opencitieslab`](https://github.com/opencitieslab) and [`opendatadurban`](https://github.com/opendatadurban) GitHub organisations. Older, archived repositories may no longer receive fixes.
